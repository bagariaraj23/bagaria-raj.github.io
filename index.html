Summer of Bitcoin 2024

Screening for Summer of Bitcoin, February to May 2024. After the seminar, the assignment was to take about 8,000 mempool transactions, drop the invalid ones, pack the rest into a block, and mine it. Bitcoin validation libraries were not allowed.

The work followed Grokking Bitcoin, from hashes and signatures through transactions, blocks, proof of work, fees, and SegWit, and the transaction and mining chapters of Mastering Bitcoin. Each transaction was checked by hand-written rules for its inputs, outputs, and signatures. Higher-fee transactions that still fit the block were kept. A coinbase transaction and merkle root went into the header, and the miner searched for a hash under the target. The assignment scored among the highest, and a project proposal was submitted. The final round did not select it.
